## 1. Purpose

This Integrated Safety, Health, Environment and Quality Policy Statement summarises Ax4 Consulting Pty Ltd’s corporate commitment to safe, lawful, environmentally responsible and technically defensible consulting work.

It is intended for tender submissions, prequalification packs, capability statements, client onboarding material and internal orientation. It should be read with the full Ax4 Work Health and Safety Policy, Environmental Policy and Quality Policy.

---

## 2. Director Statement

Ax4 Consulting Pty Ltd, trading as Ax4, Ax4 Asbestos Consultants and Ax4 WHS Consultants, is committed to delivering professional asbestos, hazardous materials and WHS consulting services in a manner that protects people, supports clients, manages environmental risk and produces accurate, defensible documentation.

Ax4 recognises that our work may involve attendance at workplaces, residential properties, commercial buildings, schools, aged care facilities, hospitality sites, industrial sites, government assets and other environments where asbestos-containing materials, suspected hazardous materials, access hazards, building services, contractors, occupants and live operations may be present.

We are committed to planning and undertaking our work so far as is reasonably practicable in accordance with applicable South Australian WHS requirements, SafeWork SA guidance, Safe Work Australia Codes of Practice, relevant asbestos and hazardous materials guidance, client requirements and good professional practice.

Ax4’s objective is simple: make compliance clear, evidence-based, practical and defensible.

---

## 3. Integrated SHEQ Commitments

Ax4 is committed to:

1. providing and maintaining safe systems of work for Ax4 workers, contractors and persons who may be affected by Ax4 activities;
2. identifying hazards, assessing risks and applying suitable controls before and during work;
3. supporting stop-work authority where work is unsafe, unclear or not adequately controlled;
4. consulting and communicating with workers, contractors, clients and other relevant duty holders where SHEQ matters affect the work;
5. ensuring personnel and contractors are competent for the work they perform;
6. managing asbestos and hazardous materials risks through clear scope definition, careful inspection practice, controlled sampling where required, accurate records and appropriate escalation;
7. preventing pollution, minimising environmental harm and managing waste or contamination risks responsibly;
8. preparing reports, registers, management plans and advice that are accurate, evidence-based, technically responsible and legally defensible within the agreed scope and limitations;
9. maintaining controlled documents, templates, records and review processes;
10. reporting, reviewing and correcting incidents, hazards, near misses, environmental events, quality issues and nonconformances;
11. continually improving Ax4’s SHEQ system, field controls, templates, client communication and technical outputs.

---

## 4. Safety and Health

Ax4 will conduct its work in a manner that protects health and safety so far as is reasonably practicable.

This includes planning work, reviewing site information, identifying hazards, applying the hierarchy of control, using suitable PPE and RPE where required, following site rules, stopping unsafe work and escalating uncontrolled risks.

Ax4 workers and contractors must take reasonable care for their own health and safety, take reasonable care not to adversely affect others, follow reasonable safety instructions and report hazards, incidents and near misses promptly.

---

## 5. Environment

Ax4 will take reasonable and practicable steps to prevent pollution, protect client sites and minimise environmental harm associated with its activities.

Environmental controls may include avoiding unnecessary disturbance, preventing the spread of dust or debris, containing samples and contaminated items, managing asbestos-contaminated waste appropriately where relevant, responding to spills and notifying clients of environmental hazards identified during Ax4 work.

Ax4 does not generally act as an asbestos removal contractor. Where removal, decontamination, disposal, clearance or specialist environmental control is required, Ax4 will recommend engagement of appropriately licensed and competent persons.

---

## 6. Quality

Ax4 is committed to accurate, consistent, technically sound and defensible consulting work.

Quality begins at scope definition and continues through site planning, inspection, evidence capture, sampling decisions, register preparation, risk assessment, report writing, review, issue and record retention.

Ax4 will not overstate inspection findings, imply that all asbestos or hazardous materials have been identified, or declare sites asbestos-free where the scope and evidence do not support that conclusion. Reports and advice must clearly state scope, assumptions, exclusions, limitations and recommended actions.

---

## 7. Responsibilities

Directors and management are responsible for providing leadership, resources and oversight for Ax4’s SHEQ system.

Workers, consultants and contractors are responsible for following applicable policies, procedures, site requirements and reasonable instructions, working within their competence, reporting hazards or issues and stopping work where required.

Clients and other duty holders remain responsible for their own legal duties, workplace management obligations and implementation of required controls unless otherwise expressly agreed in writing and within Ax4’s lawful scope of service.
