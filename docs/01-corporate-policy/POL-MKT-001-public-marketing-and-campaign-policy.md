## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the controlled public positioning, target-audience architecture, campaign rotation logic and claim boundaries for AX4 marketing.

It records the August 2026 strategic pivot from a fixed general-education social calendar toward evidence-led acquisition campaigns focused on higher-value property decision pathways.

The original August 2026 **“Clear Before the Next Step”** campaign remains a valid **content source library**. It is **not** the governing sequential deployment order where later market intelligence, public deployment or Commander direction has superseded that sequence.

## 2. Core Public Promise

AX4 marketing should consistently lead toward this operating proposition:

```text
inspect the property
→ establish the evidence
→ explain the scope and limitations
→ deliver current, accessible and defensible documentation
→ support the next property decision
```

Registers, management plans, reports, sampling, QR access and contractor information are outcomes or tools within this evidence pathway. They are not isolated marketing topics to be repeated without a clear audience decision or commercial use case.

Preferred positioning language includes:

```text
current, accessible and defensible asbestos documentation
clear scope, evidence, limitations and next steps
practical asbestos information before work or property decisions
contractor-ready access where implemented
South Australian property context
```

## 3. Current Acquisition Pillars

AX4 social and public-content campaigns should rotate through four primary acquisition pillars.

| Pillar | Primary audience | Decision / pain point | Primary commercial pathway |
|---|---|---|---|
| STRATA | Strata managers, body corporate committees, owners corporations | Common-property scope, old registers, committee questions, contractor access | New register, review/reinspection, management plan, QR access |
| PROPERTY | Commercial property managers, facilities managers, landlords, portfolio managers | Maintenance pressure, portfolio drift, register currency, contractor and tenant coordination | Register/review program, portfolio compliance, pre-works check, QR access |
| TRANSACTION | Sellers, agents, buyers, conveyancers, finance/due-diligence stakeholders | Uncertainty before listing, negotiation, settlement, refinance or due diligence | Pre-purchase, pre-sale, pre-finance/due-diligence inspection |
| PROJECT | Builders, developers, project managers, owners planning works | Stop-work risk, unknown scope, disturbance, refurbishment/demolition planning | Refurbishment/pre-demolition survey, targeted assessment, sampling |

**Commercial property** is a cross-cutting market and may appear within PROPERTY, PROJECT and TRANSACTION campaigns where the property decision genuinely fits.

## 4. Campaign Rotation Doctrine

Campaigns should operate as short audience-specific mini-series rather than an endless sequence of generic asbestos education.

Default pattern:

```text
select audience
→ identify decision pressure
→ expose the evidence gap
→ explain the practical next step
→ route to the matching AX4 service surface
→ observe response
→ rotate or deepen based on evidence
```

Recommended rotation is generally **3–6 posts per pillar** before reassessing saturation and commercial response.

Do not publish repeated variants of the same proposition merely to satisfy a pre-existing calendar.

## 7. Mandatory Claim and Compliance Controls

### 7.1 Legal and statutory claims

Do not create a legal duty merely to strengthen marketing copy.

Use cautious, context-specific wording such as:

```text
may apply
depends on the property, workplace, scope and circumstances
AX4 can review the site context and advise on an appropriate assessment pathway
```

Do not state that every strata property, residential sale, commercial property or transaction requires a particular asbestos service unless that proposition is verified for the actual context.

### 7.2 Form 1 / conveyancing

Do **not** publish claims that AX4 asbestos reporting is a mandatory **Form 1 asbestos compliance** requirement unless the precise South Australian legal position has been independently verified and approved for public use.

The approved transaction framing is evidence-led:

```text
support due-diligence discussions with evidence
reduce asbestos uncertainty before listing, negotiation, settlement or finance decisions
provide clear scope, findings, limitations and practical next steps
```

### 7.3 NATA wording

AX4 must not be described as a NATA-accredited laboratory unless that becomes factually true and is formally approved.

Approved wording:

```text
samples are analysed by a NATA-accredited laboratory where required
NATA-accredited laboratory analysis
```

### 7.4 Material identification

Do not imply from an image, building age or appearance alone that a pictured material contains asbestos.

Use the appropriate evidence status:

```text
suspect material
assumed / presumed where the assessment method supports that status
sampled / analysed where laboratory evidence exists
identified ACM only where the evidence supports that conclusion
```

### 7.5 Inspection limitations

A visual or non-intrusive inspection must not be represented as proving that concealed asbestos is absent.

Marketing should preserve the distinction between:

```text
non-intrusive transaction / management inspection
vs
refurbishment or pre-demolition investigation where disturbance is planned
```

### 7.6 Turnaround and priority pathways

Do not publish unconditional turnaround guarantees.

Priority or settlement-oriented pathways may be described only as a process for confirming deadline, access, laboratory timing and deliverable practicality.

### 7.7 QR capability

QR access supports accessibility and version control. It does not replace the underlying register, management plan, review obligation, site briefing or contractor communication.

Do not claim contractor access logging, live portal features or automated compliance updating on every package unless those functions are implemented for the specific offering.

### 7.8 Proof, testimonials and client names

Do not invent testimonials, client names, accreditations, project outcomes, partner claims or usage statistics.

Any proof point must be source-verified and approved before publication.

## 8. Social Creative System

Unless a campaign-specific creative direction supersedes it, AX4 social creative should remain:

- calm, technical and documentary rather than alarmist;
- mobile-first, typically 4:5 for feed creative;
- white / warm-grey base;
- deep navy, muted green and restrained ochre accents;
- strong hierarchy and generous negative space;
- real property / inspection context where authorised;
- scope and limitations treated as trust signals;
- no hazard-tape theatre;
- no AI-generated imitation of the AX4 logo;
- no client or site identifiers without approval.

## 11. Intelligence-Driven Pivot Rule

A campaign calendar is a plan, not immutable authority.

A pivot is permitted when evidence indicates a materially stronger audience, trigger, message or acquisition path, provided:

1. the evidence or operational observation is recorded;
2. unsupported claims are not introduced;
3. the new direction remains inside this policy;
4. material changes to legal/compliance wording are reviewed before publication;
5. the current campaign state is updated so later agents do not resume a superseded sequence.

## 12. Supersession and Continuity Rule

For August 2026 campaign work:

```text
original “Clear Before the Next Step” calendar
= content bank / historical campaign plan

current four-pillar acquisition doctrine
= governing strategic deployment model
```

A later dated Commander-approved marketing policy or orchestration alignment may supersede this document. Supersession must be explicit; historical campaign files are not deleted merely because their deployment order is no longer current.
