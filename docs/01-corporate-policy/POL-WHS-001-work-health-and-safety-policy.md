## 1. Purpose

Ax4 Consulting Pty Ltd, trading as Ax4, Ax4 Asbestos Consultants and Ax4 WHS Consultants, is committed to providing and maintaining a safe and healthy working environment, so far as is reasonably practicable, for workers, contractors, clients, visitors and other persons who may be affected by Ax4 work.

This policy sets Ax4’s corporate work health and safety commitment across asbestos consulting, hazardous materials consulting, WHS consulting, site inspections, surveys, sampling coordination, register updates, management plan work, contractor liaison, report preparation and related field activities.

Ax4 recognises that its work may involve attendance at sites where asbestos-containing materials, suspected asbestos-containing materials, asbestos-contaminated dust or debris, lead-containing materials, mould-affected materials, synthetic mineral fibre, confined or restricted spaces, roof spaces, electrical services, plant rooms, traffic areas and other hazards may be present. Ax4 will plan, perform and review its work in a manner that eliminates or minimises risks so far as is reasonably practicable.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Ax4 directors, officers, workers, contractors, consultants and any person undertaking work on behalf of Ax4;
- work performed at residential, commercial, industrial, education, aged care, government, hospitality and mixed-use sites;
- asbestos inspections, asbestos register work, asbestos management plan work, hazardous materials assessments, sampling activities, site meetings, contractor liaison and reporting activities;
- office-based, field-based, travel-related and client-facing work activities;
- persons who may be affected by Ax4 work, including clients, occupants, contractors, visitors and members of the public.

This policy should be read with Ax4’s Environmental Policy, Quality Policy, SHEQ Management Manual, Site Safety, Health and Environment Plan, Job Safety Analysis, Safe Work Method Statements, incident reporting process, asbestos inspection and sampling procedures, contractor management procedure and any applicable client or site-specific requirements.

---

## 3. Policy Statement

Ax4 is committed to:

1. providing and maintaining a working environment without risks to health and safety, so far as is reasonably practicable;
2. complying with applicable work health and safety legislation, regulations, codes of practice and site requirements;
3. identifying hazards, assessing risks and implementing suitable controls before and during work;
4. applying the hierarchy of control when managing WHS risks;
5. maintaining safe systems of work for asbestos and hazardous materials consulting activities;
6. ensuring workers and contractors have appropriate information, instruction, training and supervision;
7. consulting with workers and contractors on matters that may affect their health and safety;
8. supporting stop-work authority where work is unsafe or risks are not adequately controlled;
9. reporting, investigating and reviewing incidents, hazards and near misses;
10. continually improving Ax4’s WHS management system, documents and field controls.

## 4. Legal and Other Compliance Obligations

Ax4 will comply with applicable WHS and asbestos-related legal duties relevant to its work.

Relevant obligations and guidance may include:

- Work Health and Safety Act 2012 (SA);
- Work Health and Safety Regulations 2012 (SA), including asbestos-related requirements where relevant;
- SafeWork SA guidance;
- Safe Work Australia Codes of Practice, including asbestos-related Codes of Practice;
- relevant Australian Standards where applicable to the work;
- client site rules, induction requirements, permit systems and contractor management requirements;
- applicable environmental, waste and hazardous materials requirements where they overlap with WHS controls.

Ax4 will monitor changes to relevant legal and other requirements and update this policy or related procedures where required.

## 5. WHS Duties and Responsibilities

### 5.1 Directors and Officers

Ax4 directors and officers are responsible for exercising due diligence and providing leadership for WHS management.

This includes taking reasonable steps to:

- understand the WHS risks associated with Ax4 operations;
- ensure appropriate resources and processes are available to eliminate or minimise risks;
- ensure WHS information is received, considered and acted on;
- ensure incidents, hazards and near misses are reported and reviewed;
- verify that WHS processes are implemented and effective;
- support workers and contractors to stop work where risks are not adequately controlled.

### 5.2 Managers and Supervisors

Managers, supervisors and persons coordinating Ax4 work are responsible for:

- planning work so hazards and controls are identified before site attendance;
- ensuring workers and contractors are competent for assigned work;
- ensuring site-specific requirements, inductions, permits and access conditions are understood;
- ensuring suitable PPE, RPE, tools, equipment and documents are available;
- monitoring field work and responding to changed conditions;
- reviewing incidents, hazards, near misses and corrective actions;
- escalating matters that require client, contractor, hygienist, assessor, removalist or regulator involvement.

### 5.3 Workers and Consultants

Workers and consultants are responsible for:

- taking reasonable care for their own health and safety;
- taking reasonable care that their acts or omissions do not adversely affect others;
- following Ax4 procedures, site rules, JSA/SHEP/SWMS controls and reasonable instructions;
- using PPE, RPE and equipment correctly;
- reporting hazards, incidents, near misses, uncontrolled risks and changed site conditions;
- stopping work where the work cannot be performed safely;
- not disturbing suspected asbestos-containing materials, hazardous materials or contaminated areas outside the agreed scope and controls.

### 5.4 Contractors and Subcontractors

Contractors and subcontractors engaged by or working with Ax4 must:

- comply with applicable WHS laws, site rules and Ax4 requirements;
- hold relevant licences, competencies and insurances where required;
- provide SWMS, JSA, permits, licences, certificates or other evidence where relevant;
- report incidents, hazards, near misses and changed conditions;
- manage risks arising from their own work;
- cooperate with Ax4, clients and other duty holders.

## 6. Hazard Identification, Risk Assessment and Control

Ax4 will identify hazards, assess risks and implement controls before and during work.

Hazard identification may occur through:

- quote and scope review;
- client information and site history review;
- asbestos registers, hazardous materials registers and prior reports;
- site inductions and pre-start discussions;
- SHEP, JSA, SWMS and dynamic risk assessment processes;
- worker observations and field notes;
- incident and near miss reviews;
- client, contractor or duty holder notifications.

Ax4 will apply the hierarchy of control when selecting risk controls, with preference given to eliminating risks where practicable, followed by substitution, isolation, engineering controls, administrative controls and PPE/RPE.

Where risks cannot be adequately controlled, work must not proceed until the risk is reassessed and suitable controls are implemented.

---

## 7. Stop-Work Authority

All Ax4 workers and contractors have authority to stop work where they reasonably believe the work presents an uncontrolled or unacceptable risk to health, safety or the environment.

Stop-work triggers may include:

- suspected asbestos disturbance or uncontrolled asbestos-containing dust or debris;
- discovery of unexpected hazardous materials;
- unsafe access, unstable structures, fragile surfaces or fall risks;
- electrical, plant, confined space, traffic or live service risks that are not controlled;
- inadequate PPE/RPE, equipment, supervision, information or permits;
- client or contractor instructions that conflict with safe work requirements;
- any condition where the worker is uncertain whether work can proceed safely.

Where work is stopped, the worker must make the area safe if safe to do so, notify the relevant Ax4 contact and site contact, record the issue and wait for reassessment before recommencing work.

No worker or contractor will be penalised for stopping work in good faith due to a health and safety concern.

## 8. Consultation and Communication

Ax4 will consult, cooperate and coordinate with workers, contractors, clients and other duty holders where WHS matters may affect the work.

Consultation and communication may include:

- pre-start discussions;
- site inductions;
- job planning and scope clarification;
- review of asbestos registers, hazardous materials registers and site information;
- discussion of access limitations, sampling limitations and intrusive inspection requirements;
- contractor coordination and SWMS/JSA review;
- incident, hazard and near miss reporting;
- toolbox discussions or project briefings where required.

Workers and contractors are encouraged to raise WHS concerns, improvement suggestions and questions before or during work.

---

## 9. Training and Competency

Ax4 will ensure, so far as is reasonably practicable, that workers and contractors are competent to perform their assigned work safely.

Training and competency requirements may include:

- site induction and client-specific induction;
- construction induction training where required;
- asbestos awareness and asbestos-related competency appropriate to the work;
- asbestos assessor licensing where applicable to the role;
- sampling competency and technical supervision;
- respiratory protection training and fit testing where required;
- PPE/RPE selection, use, maintenance and limitations;
- working at heights, confined space, first aid, electrical awareness or other task-specific training where relevant;
- incident, hazard and near miss reporting requirements.

Training and competency evidence will be recorded and reviewed through Ax4’s training and competency register or equivalent controlled record.

---

## 10. PPE, RPE and Equipment

Ax4 will provide, require or verify the availability of PPE, RPE and equipment suitable for the work being performed.

This may include:

- safety footwear;
- high visibility clothing;
- eye protection;
- gloves;
- disposable coveralls;
- respiratory protective equipment;
- hearing protection;
- first aid equipment;
- sample collection materials;
- waste containment materials;
- lighting, access equipment or other field equipment where required.

PPE and RPE must be selected based on risk, site requirements and the nature of the work. PPE and RPE are not a substitute for higher-order controls where those controls are reasonably practicable.

Workers must use PPE and RPE correctly and report damaged, unsuitable or missing equipment before work proceeds.

---

## 11. Asbestos and Hazardous Materials Risk Control

Ax4’s work may involve identification, assessment, sampling coordination, register updates and management planning for asbestos and other hazardous materials.

Ax4 will manage asbestos and hazardous materials risk by:

- defining the agreed scope of work before attendance;
- reviewing existing registers, reports and site information where available;
- recognising that visual inspections cannot identify all concealed asbestos or hazardous materials;
- treating suspected asbestos-containing materials as asbestos-containing unless confirmed otherwise;
- ensuring sampling is performed only where within scope, safe, practicable and approved where required;
- recording access limitations, no-access areas and assumptions;
- avoiding unnecessary disturbance of suspected hazardous materials;
- escalating higher-risk conditions to the client or relevant duty holder;
- recommending licensed removalists, hygienists, assessors, laboratories or other competent persons where required;
- ensuring report wording remains technically defensible and does not imply that a site is asbestos-free.

Ax4 does not assume the client’s duty-holder obligations unless expressly agreed in writing and within Ax4’s lawful scope of service. The client or relevant person with management or control of the workplace remains responsible for implementing required controls, register management, access control, removal works and ongoing site management unless otherwise agreed.

## 12. Incident, Hazard and Near Miss Reporting

All Ax4 workers and contractors must report incidents, hazards and near misses as soon as practicable.

Reportable WHS matters may include:

- injury, illness or suspected exposure;
- asbestos disturbance or suspected asbestos exposure;
- uncontrolled dust, debris or contamination;
- unsafe access, falls, slips, trips or structural concerns;
- electrical, plant, traffic, confined space or live service hazards;
- failure of PPE, RPE, equipment or controls;
- client or contractor activities that create risk;
- any condition that causes work to stop or change.

Incident and hazard records should include:

- date, time and location;
- persons involved;
- description of the event or hazard;
- immediate controls applied;
- client or site contact notified;
- photographs where appropriate;
- corrective actions;
- close-out verification.

Incidents will be reviewed to identify corrective actions, training needs, procedural improvements and opportunities to prevent recurrence.

Notifiable incidents must be escalated and managed in accordance with applicable WHS legal requirements.

---

## 13. Emergency Preparedness

Ax4 will plan for foreseeable emergency situations associated with its work.

Emergency preparedness may include:

- identifying emergency contacts and nearest medical support where required;
- identifying first aid arrangements;
- confirming evacuation and assembly point requirements where available;
- maintaining field access to emergency contact information;
- carrying suitable first aid equipment where required;
- following client or site emergency procedures;
- responding to asbestos disturbance, spill, injury, exposure, fire, traffic or access emergencies in accordance with applicable procedures.

Workers must prioritise personal safety and the safety of others during an emergency and must contact emergency services where required.

---

## 14. Contractor and Partner Management

Ax4 may work with or coordinate removalists, hygienists, assessors, laboratories, consultants, trades and other technical partners.

Ax4 will, where relevant:

- verify licences, competencies and insurances;
- confirm scope and responsibility boundaries;
- request or review SWMS, JSA, permits or supporting documents;
- communicate known site hazards and limitations;
- require incidents and hazards to be reported;
- retain relevant evidence, such as clearance documentation, waste records, laboratory reports, licences or certificates.

Contractor engagement does not remove Ax4’s responsibility to manage its own workers or work activities. Contractors remain responsible for the safety and compliance of their own work.

---

## 15. Monitoring, Review and Continuous Improvement

Ax4 will monitor and review WHS performance to improve its system and controls.

Review activities may include:

- review of incidents, hazards and near misses;
- review of SHEP, JSA and SWMS effectiveness;
- review of training and competency records;
- review of client feedback and contractor performance;
- review of legislative or code changes;
- quality review of reports and risk wording;
- periodic management review of the WHS/SHEQ system.

This policy will be reviewed:

- at least every two years;
- following significant legislative or regulatory change;
- following a significant incident or near miss;
- following changes to Ax4’s scope of services;
- where audits, client feedback or operational reviews identify a need for improvement.
