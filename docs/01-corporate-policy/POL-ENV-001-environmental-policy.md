## 1. Purpose

Ax4 Consulting Pty Ltd, trading as Ax4, Ax4 Asbestos Consultants and Ax4 WHS Consultants, is committed to conducting its work in a manner that protects the environment, prevents pollution, manages waste responsibly and complies with applicable legal, regulatory, client and site requirements.

This policy sets Ax4’s corporate environmental commitment across asbestos consulting, hazardous materials consulting, WHS consulting, site inspections, surveys, sampling coordination, register updates, management plan work, contractor liaison, report preparation and related field activities.

Ax4 recognises that its work may involve sites where asbestos-containing materials, suspected asbestos-containing materials, asbestos-contaminated dust or debris, lead-containing materials, synthetic mineral fibre, mould-affected materials, deteriorated building products, contaminated waste or other environmental hazards may be present. Ax4 will take reasonable and practicable steps to prevent or minimise environmental harm arising from its activities.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Ax4 directors, workers, contractors, consultants and any person undertaking work on behalf of Ax4;
- work performed at residential, commercial, industrial, education, aged care, government, hospitality and mixed-use sites;
- asbestos inspections, asbestos register work, asbestos management plan work, hazardous materials assessments, sampling activities, site meetings, contractor liaison and reporting activities;
- environmental risks associated with dust, debris, waste, asbestos-contaminated waste, spills, site disturbance, vehicle use, sample handling and site protection.

This policy should be read with Ax4’s Work Health and Safety Policy, Quality Policy, SHEQ Management Manual, Site Safety, Health and Environment Plan, Job Safety Analysis, incident reporting process, asbestos inspection and sampling procedures, waste control procedures and any applicable client or site-specific environmental requirements.

---

## 3. Policy Statement

Ax4 is committed to:

1. preventing pollution and minimising environmental harm so far as is reasonably practicable;
2. managing waste, including asbestos-contaminated waste where relevant, in a safe and lawful manner;
3. protecting client sites from avoidable contamination, damage or disturbance;
4. preventing spills and responding promptly where a spill or accidental release occurs;
5. notifying clients of environmental hazards identified during Ax4 work;
6. complying with applicable environmental, WHS, asbestos and hazardous materials requirements;
7. applying sustainable work practices where practicable;
8. reporting and reviewing environmental incidents, hazards and near misses;
9. continually improving environmental management practices.

---

## 4. Pollution Prevention

Ax4 will plan and conduct work to prevent pollution to air, soil, stormwater, buildings, plant, equipment and surrounding areas.

Where Ax4 activities may disturb dust, debris, asbestos-containing materials, suspected asbestos-containing materials, lead-containing materials, mould-affected materials, synthetic mineral fibre or other hazardous substances, Ax4 will consider appropriate controls before work proceeds.

Pollution prevention controls may include:

- limiting disturbance during inspection and sampling activities;
- using controlled sampling methods where sampling is within the agreed scope;
- avoiding unnecessary breakage or damage to suspect materials;
- using appropriate personal protective equipment and respiratory protective equipment;
- preventing the spread of dust, fibres, fragments or debris;
- isolating or restricting access to affected areas where required;
- stopping work where conditions are unsafe or environmentally unacceptable;
- recommending escalation to the client where licensed removal, hygienist support, air monitoring, decontamination or further assessment is required.

Ax4 workers and contractors must not knowingly create uncontrolled contamination or allow contaminated material to be spread from an affected area.

---

## 5. Waste Control

Ax4 will minimise waste generation where practicable and ensure waste generated by Ax4 activities is managed safely and lawfully.

Waste control requirements include:

- avoiding unnecessary generation of contaminated waste;
- segregating waste where practicable;
- preventing uncontrolled release of dust, fibres, fragments or residues;
- ensuring contaminated disposable PPE is appropriately contained;
- ensuring bulk samples are sealed and transported in a manner that prevents release or cross-contamination;
- not placing asbestos-contaminated materials, contaminated PPE or asbestos waste into general waste streams;
- using licensed or approved waste facilities where asbestos waste disposal is required;
- retaining relevant waste, disposal, clearance or contractor records where they form part of the project evidence trail.

Ax4 does not generally act as an asbestos removal contractor. Where waste removal, decontamination, licensed asbestos removal, transport or disposal is required beyond Ax4’s consulting scope, Ax4 will recommend that the client engage appropriately licensed and competent persons.

---

## 6. Asbestos-Contaminated Waste

Where asbestos-contaminated waste, asbestos-containing debris or contaminated disposable PPE is encountered or generated during Ax4 work, Ax4 personnel must:

- avoid unnecessary disturbance;
- prevent tracking or spread from the affected area;
- use suitable PPE and RPE based on the assessed risk;
- seal samples or minor contaminated items appropriately;
- treat suspected asbestos-contaminated waste as asbestos-containing until confirmed otherwise;
- notify the client where waste, debris, contamination or deterioration requires management action;
- escalate to licensed asbestos removal, decontamination or clearance personnel where required.

Ax4 must not represent a site or area as asbestos-free. Where asbestos has not been identified during a visual inspection, that finding must be read in light of the agreed scope, access limitations, sampling limitations and the possibility of hidden or inaccessible asbestos-containing materials.

---

## 7. Site Protection

Ax4 will respect and protect client sites during all work activities.

Site protection includes:

- leaving inspected areas in a safe and tidy condition;
- avoiding unnecessary disturbance to buildings, finishes, plant, equipment, grounds and occupied areas;
- using care when accessing roof spaces, plant rooms, service areas, basements, risers, ceiling spaces, external areas and restricted spaces;
- avoiding contamination of clean areas;
- maintaining client access, business continuity and occupant safety where practicable;
- reporting observed environmental hazards that may affect occupants, workers, contractors or future works.

Where site conditions present an environmental or contamination risk outside Ax4’s scope or control, Ax4 will notify the client and provide reasonable recommendations within its area of expertise.

---

## 8. Spill Prevention and Response

Ax4 will take reasonable steps to prevent spills or accidental release of substances during its activities.

Spill prevention measures may include:

- keeping liquids, chemicals, fuels or sample materials secure during transport;
- avoiding storage of materials near stormwater drains, waterways, soil, gardens or sensitive areas;
- carrying spill response equipment where required by the nature of the work;
- following client site spill procedures where applicable;
- stopping work where a spill or release creates a safety or environmental risk.

In the event of a spill or accidental release, Ax4 personnel must:

1. stop work if safe to do so;
2. protect people first;
3. prevent further release or spread where practicable;
4. isolate the affected area if required;
5. notify the client or site contact;
6. notify emergency services or regulators where required;
7. record the incident through Ax4’s incident reporting process;
8. review corrective actions before work recommences.

---

## 9. Client Notification of Environmental Hazards

Ax4 will notify the client, site contact or relevant duty holder where environmental hazards are identified during Ax4 work.

Examples may include:

- suspected asbestos-containing dust or debris;
- damaged or deteriorated asbestos-containing materials;
- uncontrolled waste or suspected incorrect waste handling;
- suspected lead paint, mould, synthetic mineral fibre, chemical residue, contaminated soil or other hazardous materials;
- evidence of uncontrolled dust release;
- poor waste storage or incorrect asbestos waste handling;
- spills, leaks, water damage or conditions that may increase environmental risk;
- conditions that may affect planned maintenance, demolition, refurbishment or contractor works.

Client notification may be verbal for immediate risk control. Significant issues should be recorded in writing through field notes, email, report findings, register updates or incident records.

Client notification does not transfer Ax4 into the role of the client’s duty holder, principal contractor, asbestos removalist or site controller unless expressly agreed in writing and within Ax4’s lawful scope of service.

## 10. Legal and Other Compliance Obligations

Ax4 will comply with applicable environmental, WHS, asbestos and hazardous materials duties relevant to its work.

Relevant obligations and guidance may include:

- Environment Protection Act 1993 (SA);
- Environment Protection Regulations 2023 (SA);
- Work Health and Safety Act 2012 (SA);
- Work Health and Safety Regulations 2012 (SA), including asbestos-related requirements where relevant;
- SafeWork SA guidance;
- Safe Work Australia asbestos Codes of Practice;
- applicable Australian Standards where relevant to the work;
- EPA South Australia waste and pollution requirements;
- client site environmental management requirements;
- licensed waste facility requirements for asbestos-contaminated waste.

Ax4 will monitor changes to relevant legal and other requirements and update this policy or related procedures where required.

---

## 11. Sustainable Work Practices

Ax4 will apply sustainable work practices where practicable and appropriate to the nature of the work.

This may include:

- reducing unnecessary travel through efficient job scheduling;
- using electronic reports and records where accepted by clients;
- minimising printing unless hard copies are required;
- using durable equipment and maintaining it properly;
- reducing unnecessary sampling while maintaining technical confidence, safety and compliance;
- planning inspections to avoid repeat site visits where possible;
- reusing controlled templates and digital systems to reduce administrative waste;
- supporting clients to maintain accurate registers and management plans, reducing uncontrolled disturbance during future works.

Sustainable practices must not compromise safety, legal compliance, sampling integrity, report quality, technical defensibility or environmental protection.

---

## 12. Environmental Incident Reporting

All Ax4 workers and contractors must report environmental incidents, hazards, near misses or uncontrolled releases as soon as practicable.

Reportable environmental matters may include:

- release or spread of asbestos-containing dust, debris, fibres or contaminated material;
- incorrect disposal or suspected incorrect disposal of asbestos-contaminated waste;
- spills to soil, stormwater, drains, waterways or sensitive areas;
- damage to materials creating contamination risk;
- discovery of uncontrolled hazardous materials;
- failure of environmental controls;
- client or contractor activity that may create environmental harm;
- any event requiring regulator, client or emergency notification.

Environmental incident records should include:

- date, time and location;
- persons involved;
- description of the event;
- immediate controls applied;
- client or site contact notified;
- photographs where appropriate;
- waste or contamination management actions;
- corrective actions;
- close-out verification.

Incidents will be reviewed to identify corrective actions, training needs, procedural improvements and opportunities to prevent recurrence.

## 13. Responsibilities

### 13.1 Directors and Management

Ax4 directors and management are responsible for:

- providing leadership and resources for environmental protection;
- maintaining this policy and related procedures;
- ensuring workers understand environmental expectations;
- reviewing incidents and corrective actions;
- ensuring environmental risks are considered during planning and quoting;
- ensuring contractors or partner consultants are appropriately competent for their work.

### 13.2 Workers and Consultants

Workers and consultants are responsible for:

- following this policy and relevant procedures;
- protecting client sites and the environment;
- reporting hazards, incidents, near misses and uncontrolled conditions;
- applying pollution prevention and waste controls;
- stopping work where an environmental risk cannot be adequately controlled;
- notifying management where client instructions may conflict with environmental or legal duties.

### 13.3 Contractors and Subcontractors

Contractors and subcontractors engaged by or working with Ax4 must:

- comply with applicable legislation and site requirements;
- hold relevant licences, competencies and insurances where required;
- manage waste and environmental risks arising from their work;
- report incidents or hazards to Ax4 and/or the client;
- provide records, certificates, disposal dockets, clearance documentation or other evidence where relevant to the work.

---

## 14. Monitoring and Review

This policy will be reviewed:

- at least every two years;
- following significant legislative or regulatory change;
- following a significant environmental incident;
- following changes to Ax4’s scope of services;
- where audits, client feedback or operational reviews identify a need for improvement.
