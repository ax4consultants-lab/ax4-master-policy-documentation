## 1. Purpose

This template provides Ax4 Consulting Pty Ltd with a controlled Safe Work Method Statement (SWMS) format for work activities that involve high risk construction work or where a client, principal contractor or site controller requires a SWMS before work commences.

The SWMS is intended to identify the high risk construction work, hazards and risks, selected control measures, implementation method, monitoring arrangements and worker sign-on requirements for the specific work activity.

This template is not a substitute for a site-specific risk assessment. It must be reviewed and completed for the actual work, location, personnel, access conditions, client requirements and foreseeable hazards.

---

## 2. Scope

This template may be used for Ax4 field work and related consulting activities, including where relevant:

- asbestos and hazardous materials inspections;
- visual inspections and surveys;
- sampling-related activities where approved and safe to perform;
- work in or near construction or refurbishment areas;
- work near traffic, plant, electrical services, ceiling spaces, plant rooms or restricted access areas;
- work requiring coordination with a client, principal contractor, licensed removalist, hygienist, laboratory, facilities manager or other duty holder.

A task-specific SWMS must be prepared where Ax4 is carrying out, or is directly involved in, high risk construction work. Where Ax4 is not performing the high risk construction work but may be affected by it, Ax4 workers must review relevant site SWMS, comply with site controls and stop work where conditions are unsafe.

---

## 3. When a SWMS is Required

A SWMS must be prepared before high risk construction work commences.

For Ax4 work, a SWMS may be required where the work involves, or is likely to involve, one or more high risk triggers, including but not limited to:

- risk of a person falling more than 2 metres;
- demolition affecting a load-bearing element or structural integrity;
- work that involves, or is likely to involve, disturbing asbestos;
- work in or near a confined space;
- work in or near a shaft, trench deeper than 1.5 metres, or tunnel;
- work on or near energised electrical installations or services;
- work in an area that may have a contaminated or flammable atmosphere;
- work on, in or adjacent to a traffic corridor in use by traffic other than pedestrians;
- work in an area with movement of powered mobile plant;
- work in or near water or other liquid involving a drowning risk.

If there is uncertainty about whether the work requires a SWMS, the matter must be escalated to the Director or nominated supervisor before work proceeds.

---

## 4. Responsibilities

### 4.1 Director / Responsible Manager

The Director or responsible manager must ensure, so far as is reasonably practicable, that:

- a SWMS is prepared where required;
- the SWMS is suitable for the work and site conditions;
- workers and contractors are consulted about the work method and controls;
- required competencies, licences, permits and inductions are confirmed;
- the SWMS is provided to the principal contractor or client where required;
- work is monitored for compliance with the SWMS;
- the SWMS is reviewed when conditions or work methods change.

### 4.2 Worker / Consultant

Workers and consultants must:

- read and understand the SWMS before commencing the task;
- follow the controls listed in the SWMS;
- stop work if conditions are unsafe, unclear or materially different from the SWMS;
- report hazards, incidents, near misses and changed site conditions;
- sign onto the SWMS before starting work;
- participate in review where the SWMS is revised.

### 4.3 Contractors and Partner Organisations

Contractors and partner organisations must:

- provide their own SWMS where they are performing high risk construction work under their control;
- provide evidence of licences, competencies and insurance where requested;
- follow site rules and agreed control measures;
- notify Ax4 and relevant duty holders of hazards, incidents, changed conditions or work scope changes.

## 13. Asbestos and Hazardous Materials Control Notes

Where suspect asbestos-containing material or other hazardous material is identified, workers must apply a conservative control approach.

Ax4 workers must not imply that an area, building or site is free from asbestos or other hazardous materials unless that conclusion is properly supported within the approved scope and limitations of the relevant report.

Where materials are suspected or presumed to contain asbestos, they must be treated as asbestos-containing unless laboratory analysis or reliable existing records confirm otherwise.

Sampling is not automatic. Sampling must depend on scope, access, risk, practicality, safety and client approval where applicable.

If unexpected disturbance, visible debris, damaged suspect material or uncontrolled exposure risk is identified, stop work, restrict access where safe to do so, notify the site contact and escalate to the Director or nominated supervisor.

## 15. Stop-Work Triggers

Work must stop if:

- the work area or task is materially different from the SWMS;
- required controls cannot be implemented;
- workers are asked to perform work outside competency, licence or scope;
- unexpected suspect asbestos, dust, debris or hazardous material is identified;
- a worker may be exposed to uncontrolled risk;
- live services, confined space, fall risk or structural hazards are identified but not controlled;
- an incident, near miss or uncontrolled release occurs;
- the principal contractor, client or Ax4 Director directs work to stop.

Work must not recommence until the risk has been reassessed, controls have been confirmed and the SWMS has been reviewed where required.

---

## 16. Monitoring and Review

The nominated supervisor or responsible worker must monitor whether the work is being carried out in accordance with this SWMS.

This SWMS must be reviewed if:

- there is a change to work method, location, personnel, plant or equipment;
- new hazards are identified;
- the work is not being carried out in accordance with the SWMS;
- an incident, near miss or unsafe condition occurs;
- requested by the client, principal contractor, regulator or Ax4 Director;
- a relevant law, code, guidance document or internal procedure changes.

Changes must be communicated to affected workers and contractors before work resumes.
