## 1. Purpose

This procedure defines how Ax4 Consulting Pty Ltd controls WHS/SHEQ documents, procedures, templates, forms, registers, technical libraries and related controlled records.

The purpose of document control is to ensure that Ax4 documents are current, approved, traceable, consistently named, protected from uncontrolled alteration and suitable for use in South Australian asbestos, hazardous materials and WHS consulting work.

Controlled documentation supports legal defensibility, technical consistency, tender readiness, field safety control and future automation through Atom/EchoForge workflows.

---

## 2. Scope

This procedure applies to controlled Ax4 documentation including:

- corporate policies;
- SHEQ management system documents;
- procedures;
- SHEP, JSA and SWMS templates;
- forms and registers;
- asbestos and hazardous materials technical procedures;
- report limitation libraries;
- recommended action wording libraries;
- client-facing controlled templates;
- internal governance, review and approval records.

This procedure applies to documents stored in the Ax4 master policy documentation repository, working document folders, approved template folders, issued report folders and any future controlled documentation system.

This procedure does not apply to informal working notes unless those notes are later converted into a controlled document, template, register, procedure or approved library entry.

---

## 3. Document Control Principles

Ax4 will manage controlled documents so that:

1. each controlled document has a unique document code;
2. document purpose, owner and approval authority are clear;
3. only current approved versions are used for operational work;
4. draft documents are clearly separated from approved documents;
5. superseded documents are archived and not used by mistake;
6. changes are traceable through version history and changelog records;
7. templates are protected from uncontrolled editing;
8. issued client documents are retained as records;
9. document wording remains technically accurate, legally defensible and aligned with Ax4 scope;
10. automation systems can reliably ingest, reference and reproduce approved wording.

## 5. Document Status Categories

Each controlled document must have one of the following status categories.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Not started | Document has not yet been claimed or drafted. |
| Selected / claimed | A drafting agent or responsible person has selected the document for work. |
| In progress | Drafting, research, revision or review activity has started. |
| Draft complete | Controlled draft exists and is ready for human review. |
| In review | Document is under Director, technical, legal or operational review. |
| Needs revision | Document requires correction before approval or use. |
| Approved | Document has been approved for operational use. |
| Superseded | Document has been replaced by a newer approved version. |
| Retired | Document has been withdrawn from use and not replaced. |

Draft complete does not mean approved. A document may only be used operationally when the authorised approver has approved it.

## 6. Roles and Responsibilities

### 6.1 Directors

Directors are responsible for:

- approving corporate policies and key WHS/SHEQ system documents;
- ensuring controlled documents are appropriate for Ax4 operations;
- confirming the final approval status of documents before operational release;
- resolving conflicts between documents, procedures or technical wording;
- authorising major changes to controlled documentation structure.

### 6.2 Document Owner

The document owner is responsible for:

- maintaining the assigned document;
- initiating periodic review;
- ensuring the document reflects current Ax4 practice;
- ensuring related procedures, forms and templates remain aligned;
- coordinating review input from directors, technical personnel, workers or contractors where required.

### 6.3 Drafting Agent or Author

The author or drafting agent is responsible for:

- claiming the document before starting work;
- working only on the assigned document code;
- using the approved repository structure and file naming conventions;
- preserving document separation;
- avoiding unsupported legal or technical claims;
- recording assumptions, linked documents, references and open questions;
- updating the checklist and changelog in accordance with this procedure.

### 6.4 Reviewer

The reviewer is responsible for checking that the document is:

- technically accurate;
- suitable for Ax4 operations;
- consistent with related controlled documents;
- aligned with South Australian WHS and asbestos requirements where relevant;
- free from outdated references, placeholders, broken fields or client-specific contamination.

### 6.5 Workers and Contractors

Workers and contractors must:

- use current approved documents and templates;
- not rely on superseded or draft documents for operational work unless specifically authorised;
- report errors, unclear wording or outdated content;
- follow issued procedures and forms relevant to their work.

## 7. Creating a New Controlled Document

Before creating a new controlled document, the author must:

1. confirm the document is listed in `DOCUMENT-REGISTER.md` or approved for addition by a Director;
2. confirm the correct document code and title;
3. check `COMPLETION-CHECKLIST.md` to confirm the document is not already claimed;
4. mark the document as `Selected / claimed` before drafting begins;
5. record the claim in `changelog/CHANGELOG.md`;
6. read applicable source material, roadmap requirements and agent operating rules;
7. create the draft in the correct repository folder.

New documents must include a document control table, purpose, scope, responsibilities where relevant, document history and assumptions/open review items where the document is still in draft.

## 8. Version Control Rules

Ax4 uses version numbers to identify the development and approval stage of controlled documents.

| Version Type | Use |
|---|---|
| 0.x | Early working draft or incomplete internal draft. |
| 1.0 | First controlled draft or first approved release, depending on status field. |
| 1.1, 1.2 | Minor update, correction or wording improvement. |
| 2.0 | Major revision, structural change or substantial content change. |

The version number must be read together with the status field. For example, `Version 1.0 — Draft complete` is not the same as `Version 1.0 — Approved`.

A version change is required when:

- material content is added, removed or changed;
- legal, regulatory or technical references are updated;
- responsibilities or approval authority changes;
- a template or form changes in a way that affects use;
- report wording, limitations or client-facing clauses change materially.

Minor spelling or formatting changes may be recorded in the changelog without increasing the version number, unless the document owner determines otherwise.

## 9. Approval Authority

Approval authority must be suitable to the document type and operational risk.

| Document Type | Minimum Approval Authority |
|---|---|
| Corporate policy | Director |
| SHEQ management manual | Director |
| WHS/SHEQ procedure | Director or delegated WHS Manager |
| Technical asbestos procedure | Director / Senior Asbestos Consultant or suitably competent technical reviewer |
| Field template or form | Director, WHS Manager or delegated document owner |
| Register | Document owner or delegated responsible person |
| Library wording | Director / Senior Asbestos Consultant or competent technical reviewer |

Where a document has legal, technical or high-risk operational implications, Ax4 may seek external technical, legal, hygienist or licensed assessor review before approval.

Approval must be recorded in the document control table, document history or approval block.

---

## 10. Review Frequency

Controlled documents must be reviewed at planned intervals or when triggered by change.

Minimum review expectations:

| Document Type | Review Frequency |
|---|---|
| Corporate policies | At least every 2 years |
| SHEQ management manual | At least every 2 years |
| WHS/SHEQ procedures | At least every 2 years |
| Technical asbestos procedures | At least every 2 years or following relevant regulatory/technical change |
| SHEP/JSA/SWMS templates | At least annually or after significant field incident/change |
| Forms and registers | At least every 2 years or when process changes |
| Library wording | At least annually or following legal/technical wording change |

A review must also occur when:

- legislation, regulation, code of practice or guidance changes;
- Ax4 changes its scope of services;
- an incident, near miss or audit identifies a document issue;
- client, tender or prequalification requirements expose a gap;
- field users identify unclear or impractical wording;
- automation workflows require clearer structure or controlled language.

## 11. File Naming Convention

Controlled document files must use clear, stable and machine-readable file names.

Preferred Markdown naming convention:

`DOCUMENT-CODE-document-title-in-lowercase-kebab-case.md`

Example:

`PRO-DOC-001-document-control-procedure.md`

Approved client-facing Word or PDF files should use a similar structure, with version and date where appropriate:

`PRO-DOC-001-document-control-procedure-v1.0-2026-05-27.pdf`

File names must not include:

- uncontrolled personal notes;
- ambiguous names such as `final-final`;
- client names unless the document is a client-specific issued record;
- outdated job paths or local drive artefacts;
- misleading approval status.

## 12. Repository and Folder Control

Controlled documents must be stored in the approved repository structure.

| Folder | Content |
|---|---|
| `docs/01-corporate-policy/` | Corporate WHS, environment, quality and SHEQ policy documents |
| `docs/02-system-governance/` | Management manuals, system procedures and governance registers |
| `docs/03-field-control/` | SHEP, JSA, SWMS, emergency and induction templates |
| `docs/04-technical-asbestos/` | Asbestos and hazardous materials technical procedures |
| `docs/05-forms-registers/` | Forms, registers and evidence capture tools |
| `docs/06-libraries/` | Standard wording libraries, limitations and coded action systems |
| `source-material/` | Legacy or reference material only |
| `archive/` | Superseded, retired or historical material |
| `changelog/` | Change records and build history |

Source material is not automatically approved for use. It must be reviewed, modernised and issued as a controlled document before being relied upon operationally.

## 13. Template Control

Templates must be controlled to prevent inconsistent or outdated use.

Template control requirements include:

- each template must have a document code and version;
- editable master templates must be stored in the approved template location;
- issued client documents must be saved separately from master templates;
- template changes must be reviewed and recorded;
- placeholders must be clear and removed before client issue;
- template fields must not contain client-specific artefacts from earlier jobs;
- obsolete templates must be archived or removed from active use.

Templates used for asbestos registers, management plans, field safety documents, JSA/SWMS forms and client-facing reports require particular care because outdated wording can create technical, legal or compliance risk.

---

## 14. Superseded Document Handling

When a document is replaced, the superseded version must be removed from active use and archived.

Superseded documents must be marked or stored so they cannot reasonably be mistaken for the current version.

Archive records should retain:

- document code;
- title;
- version;
- date superseded;
- reason for replacement;
- replacement document reference where applicable.

Superseded documents may be retained for audit, historical or legal defensibility purposes but must not be used as current guidance.

---

## 15. Archive Process

Documents may be archived when they are:

- superseded by a newer version;
- retired because the process is no longer used;
- replaced by another controlled document;
- retained as source material only;
- issued as final client records.

Archived documents must not be altered except to apply archive markings or metadata. If archived content is later reused, it must be reviewed and reissued through the normal controlled document process.

---

## 16. Change Control

Material changes to controlled documents must be recorded.

A material change includes:

- change to legal or regulatory references;
- change to duty holder language or responsibilities;
- change to asbestos, hazardous materials or WHS control wording;
- change to approval authority;
- change to report limitations, risk language or client-facing standard clauses;
- change to field controls, PPE/RPE requirements or incident escalation;
- change that affects how a worker, contractor or client uses the document.

Material changes must be recorded in:

- the document history table;
- `changelog/CHANGELOG.md` where the change affects the controlled suite;
- related registers or issue records where relevant.

---

## 17. Pre-Issue Audit Checklist

Before a controlled document is issued for review or approval, the author must check:

- document code is correct;
- document title matches the register;
- version and status are clear;
- owner and approver are identified;
- effective date and review date are completed or clearly marked for approval;
- document history is updated;
- scope matches the roadmap or approved purpose;
- no unrelated documents have been merged into the draft;
- Australian English is used;
- no unsupported legal claims are made;
- asbestos-related wording does not imply a site is asbestos-free;
- sampling wording does not imply sampling is always included;
- client/duty holder responsibilities are not transferred to Ax4 unless expressly agreed;
- placeholders are intentional and clearly marked;
- no client-specific contamination remains;
- no old local file path artefacts remain;
- linked documents are listed;
- assumptions and open questions are recorded;
- checklist, document register and changelog have been updated where required.

---

## 18. Approved Document Issue

A document may only be issued as approved when authorised by the nominated approver.

On approval:

1. status must be changed to `Approved`;
2. approval date must be inserted;
3. review date must be inserted;
4. document history must record approval;
5. active template or document location must be updated;
6. superseded versions must be archived;
7. affected workers, contractors or users must be notified where required.

Draft or unapproved documents may be used for discussion, review and build work only. They must not be represented as approved operational documents.

---

## 19. Control of Issued Client Records

Issued client records, including reports, registers, management plans and certificates, must be retained as records of work performed.

Issued records should not be altered after issue except through a controlled revision process.

Where a correction, update or revision is required, Ax4 must issue a revised version with clear revision identification and, where appropriate, a summary of changes.

Client records must be stored so they can be retrieved by job number, client, site, date and document type where practicable.

---

## 20. Automation and Future System Use

Ax4 may use Atom, EchoForge or other automation systems to assist with document generation, template population, wording libraries, registers and review workflows.

Automation tools may assist document production but do not replace human approval, technical review or Director authority.

Automation-ready documents should use:

- stable document codes;
- consistent headings;
- controlled wording;
- clear status fields;
- predictable file naming;
- explicit linked document references;
- documented assumptions and limitations.

Any automated output must be reviewed before client issue or operational use.
